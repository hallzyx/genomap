01 · Concept

Genome + map. Two strands that cross at every event.

The blaze strand is your effort: the events you show up for. The ink strand is the proof: the organizer's signature and the anchor on Stellar. Where they cross, a mark is made. Over time your path twists into something nobody else can copy: your builder genome.

02 · Logo

The mark: a helix that unwinds into a path.

Two woven strands (one passes under, then over). The blaze strand ends in a node: your latest mark, "you are here". In the wordmark, "ma" is blaze: the syllable that genoma and map share, the point where the two words cross.

03 · Color

Field paper, surveyor's ink and a trail blaze.

Blaze orange comes from the paint marks hikers leave on trees to signal a trail. It is the only warm voice: the path, primary actions, and what's new. Trust has its own four-state language.
