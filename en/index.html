01 / Field guide

From check-in
to track record.

The pilot starts with attendance. You show up; your organizer makes it count.

  1. 01

    Show up.

    At the event, show your individual GenoMap QR. No QR? Staff can find your profile manually.

  2. 02

    Get the record.

    An approved organizer confirms attendance and issues the credential. Students don't need to sign every check-in.

  3. 03

    Share the path.

    Send one profile link. Recruiters can see what you attended, who issued each credential, and its proof on Stellar.

03 / Wallet guide

Your wallet stays yours.

For the pilot, you'll only need to provide your public Stellar address. GenoMap won't create a wallet for you or ask for your password, private key, or recovery phrase.
